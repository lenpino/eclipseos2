SWT Step 024. The scroll bars of a canvas

Objective

Give a Canvas or a Composite made with SWT.H_SCROLL or SWT.V_SCROLL its scroll bars, with the values and the events of Windows. StyledText and the views of Eclipse scroll with them. The test case SWT024_01 (29 automatic checks) reads the screen, sets the values and sends the commands of the bars; its interactive part scrolls a grid.

Task notes

  • On Windows the bars are part of the window (WS_HSCROLL, WS_VSCROLL). Scrollable added SBS_HORZ and SBS_VERT to the style of the window, which make no bar on a window that is not a scroll bar, and ScrollBar sent its messages to its parent: there were no bars, the client area was the whole window, and getThumb() divided the range by the size in pixels.
  • Now the bar of a canvas (a Scrollable with the state CANVAS, not a shell) is a scroll bar window of PM, a child and an owned window of its parent, with the id FID_VERTSCROLL or FID_HORZSCROLL, placed on the right and at the bottom at creation and at every WM_SIZE. The client area is beside the bars that are visible; the bars are not children (they are no widgets), and are above the children in the order of the windows. Hiding a bar gives its room to the client area.
  • The values are kept by ScrollBar, with the meaning of Windows: the selection goes from the minimum to the maximum less the thumb, which is the range given to PM (SBM_SETSCROLLBAR), and the thumb is the visible part of the whole (SBM_SETTHUMBSIZE). The defaults are those of Windows (0, 100, thumb 10, increments 1 and 10).
  • The bar sends WM_VSCROLL or WM_HSCROLL to its owner, with its id in mp1: Scrollable gives it to the bar, which moves the position by the increment, the page increment or to the thumb, and sends a Selection with the detail of Windows (ARROW_UP, ARROW_DOWN, PAGE_UP, PAGE_DOWN, DRAG, none at the end of a drag). SHORT1FROMMP() is signed: the id 0x8006 has to be compared without the sign.
  • The bars of the native controls (list, text, table, tree) are not changed; Slider still computes its thumb the old way.

The behavior that differs from Windows is in difference 024.
