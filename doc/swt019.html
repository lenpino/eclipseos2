SWT Step 019. Scrolling a canvas

Objective

Implement Canvas.scroll(), which text editors use to move their contents. The test case SWT019_01 shows the window and reads the pixels of the screen before and after scrolling (10 automatic checks); its interactive part scrolls lines of text with buttons.

Task notes

  • As on Windows: the caret is hidden while the area is scrolled (it must not be copied with it), the pending paints are flushed (WinUpdateWindow, where Windows uses RedrawWindow), the area is copied with WinScrollWindow and the part of it that is not covered by the copy is invalidated (SW_INVALIDATERGN).
  • The rectangle is turned for the y of PM, and so is the vertical move: a positive destY - y (down in SWT) is a negative move in PM.
  • With all, Windows moves the children that intersect the area. The canvas moves them itself, so that the children out of the area stay whatever SW_SCROLLCHILDREN of PM would do with them.
  • The checks: the canvas paints a black square, then stops painting it and scrolls. A black square at the destination can only come from the copy, and white where it was means that the uncovered part was repainted. A wrong sign of the vertical move would put it elsewhere.
  • A mistake of the first version of the test, not of the port: scroll(30, 0, 0, 50, ...) also moves the area up 50, and the child that went from y 70 to 20 was right. The arguments are the destination and the source, not a displacement.
