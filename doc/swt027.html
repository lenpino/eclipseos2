SWT Step 027. The images of Eclipse

Objective

Draw the icons of Eclipse as they are: GIFs of 2, 4 and 5 bits with a transparent pixel. The test case SWT027_01 (28 automatic checks) loads seven icons of the plugins of Eclipse 2.0.1 (the three versions of a tool icon, from etool16, ctool16 and dtool16, and the icons of a folder, a project, a package and a jar, in tests/SWT/images/eclipse), draws them with GC.drawImage on white and on black and compares every pixel read from the screen with its ImageData; it also checks getImageData and the IMAGE_GRAY copy.

Task notes

  • Eclipse 2.0 does not make the gray tool icons with IMAGE_GRAY: each tool icon comes in three GIFs (gray, color when the mouse is over it, disabled). The dithered gray boxes of the tool bars were those GIFs drawn wrong.
  • The cause: the transparent entry of the palette of most icons is white, and the icon has an opaque white entry too. PM keeps one entry per color in a bitmap, so both became one: the bits read back had no pixel of the transparent index, and drawImage (BM_SRCTRANSPARENT, a transparency by color) left out every white pixel of the icon. On white it did not show; on any other background the opaque white parts were holes. Before the fix 8 of the 28 checks failed, all on icons with an opaque white.
  • Now Image, when it makes the bitmap, gives the transparent entry a color that no other entry has, if its color is shared. GC is not changed. A fix in GC that changed the entry when drawing could not work: the information was already lost in the bitmap.
  • Icons in buttons and tool items (SWT027_02, 4 checks read from the screen). Button copied the bitmap of its image (ROP_SRCCOPY), so the transparent part showed the color of the transparent entry: black once that entry had a color of its own. It draws with BM_SRCTRANSPARENT now, with the color that Image.internal_transparent_rgb reads from the bitmap. And a disabled button was covered with a halftone of its face color, also over the disabled image that Eclipse gives a tool item (the dtool16 GIF): the dithered boxes. ToolItem tells its button when it shows its disabled image, and the button then draws it as it is. The three checks of the tool items failed before; the one of the push button was fixed with the test (its first version read the white border as the face).
  • The tool items of Eclipse still showed black squares: JFace makes their images as icons, new Image (display, data, data.getTransparencyMask ()) (seen in a dump of the windows of Eclipse with the image of each button). Windows makes the mask with CreateBitmap (width, height, 1, 1, maskData): one bit, 0 black and 1 white, then inverts it. The port took the header of the color bitmap for the mask, so the two colors of the mask were the first two of the palette of the icon; the icons whose first color is light were black squares, the others were right (and an image of direct colors had no colors for its mask at all). The mask has a header of its own now, with black and white. SWT027_01 draws every icon a second time made this way, and SWT027_02 puts two of them in a tool bar: 8 of those checks failed before. The icons of the tests are now twelve, of 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 bits (save, print and back added).
  • Seen while reading the code, not checked yet: Image.setBackground uses the red twice when it makes the color of the transparent entry.
  • All the automatic tests from SWT010_01 to SWT026_02 still pass.
