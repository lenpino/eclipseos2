SWT Step 028. The gradients of the titles

Objective

Paint the titles of the views of Eclipse as Windows does. The test case SWT028_01 (4 automatic checks, read from the screen) checks fillGradientRectangle with one color and a CLabel with the gradient of an active view.

Task notes

  • Eclipse paints the title of an active view with a gradient from COLOR_TITLE_BACKGROUND to COLOR_TITLE_BACKGROUND_GRADIENT (0 to 50 percent) and then to COLOR_WIDGET_BACKGROUND (WorkbenchColors). PM has no color for the end of the gradient of a title, so both title colors are SYSCLR_ACTIVETITLE.
  • GC.fillGradientRectangle with two equal colors drew an outline only (drawRoundRectangle); Windows fills the rectangle. The first half of the title then kept what was in the buffer of the CLabel: green on the screen. It fills now. Its check failed before the fix.
  • COLOR_TITLE_INACTIVE_BACKGROUND_GRADIENT was the color of the text of an inactive title; it is the inactive title color now.
  • All the automatic tests from SWT010_01 to SWT027_02 still pass.
