SWT Step 012. Tables

Objective

Implement the o.e.swt.widgets.Table, TableItem and TableColumn classes. The test case SWT012_01 checks the columns, the texts of the cells, the bounds, the header and the lines, the selection (single and multiple), the events, removing, scrolling, the placement of items added before the table has a size and with the redrawing off, and the images and colors automatically, and then shows a table to be used by hand.

Task notes

The table is the container control of PM in its detail view, as Tree is in its tree view (see step 011 and lessons learned for the way the records are handled in native memory). What is new:

  • A column is a FIELDINFO (32 bytes), allocated with CM_ALLOCDETAILFIELDINFO and put in the container with CM_INSERTDETAILFIELDINFO. Its offStruct points to a slot of the record, where each cell has the pointer to its string (CFA_STRING), so a record has the extra bytes for 32 columns (the id of the item and 32 pointers).
  • The slot belongs to the column: another column that is inserted before does not move the texts. While there are no columns an implicit one shows the text of the items, and the first TableColumn takes its place, as the Windows port does.
  • Every cell of a new record points to an empty string before the record is inserted: the container paints at once and does not accept a null pointer.
  • The container with CCS_EXTENDSEL lets the selection be empty and be several items by program, and a SWT.SINGLE table is kept to one by Table. The other modes of selection cannot be empty (CCS_SINGLESEL) or select by toggling (CCS_MULTIPLESEL).
  • After a resize the container places its records wrongly (they go up by the height it gains): Table asks it to place them again right after the resize, see lessons.
  • The height of the header is the height of the window minus the top of the viewport of the records; the order of the items, for the access by index, is kept in a vector.
  • One click of the user is a burst of CN_EMPHASIS: the record that was selected is cleared, the new one is selected and the cursor moves, each with its notification. Reporting each change of the selection gave two Selection events per click (2026-11-10). The notifications only ask for a check (asyncExec), which runs when the burst is over and sends one event if the selection changed, with the item that has the cursor (the focused item of Windows). Seen on the screen: one event per click, also with Ctrl and Shift.
  • The events of the columns (2026-11-13). The titles are a child window of the container (CID_LEFTCOLTITLEWND) that does not notify a click: Display.filterMessage() takes the WM_BUTTON1CLICK of that window from the queue, and Table posts a Selection to the column under the pointer, as Windows does with HDN_ITEMCLICK. The title window does not move when the table scrolls (it paints scrolled): the column is found in the workspace, with the left of the viewport. A change of width by program is a Resize of the column and a Move of those that follow (HDN_ITEMCHANGED of Windows).
  • The container gives every field more width than its cxWidth: a margin, and a separator before every field but the first. It depends on the font: 15 and 18 pixels with the default font, 12 and 15 with 8.Helv, 20 and 24 with 14.Helvetica; the lines do not change it. The cells were 17 pixels too narrow and too far left for each column before them, which put a click on a title in the wrong column. The width of a TableColumn is now all of the column on the screen, as on Windows: Table measures the margins in a hidden container with the font of the table (one record, one and then two fields of one pixel), and gives each field the width less its margin. A cxWidth of 0 is not an empty column but one that fits its texts, so the narrowest column is the margin plus one pixel.
  • Placing the records again (CM_INVALIDATERECORD with CMA_REPOSITION, see above) also scrolls the table horizontally, back to the left or to another place: every change of the table took the user back. Table scrolls it back to where it was.
  • The direction of the scrolling is checked on the screen (2026-11-13): only two items have an image, and after setTopIndex() and showItem() its green pixels are in the first or the last row. A positive amount of CM_SCROLLWINDOW shows the lower records.
  • getImageBounds(0) gave the start of the first cell, but the image is drawn in the column of the images, before it: centered in its 22 pixels, after the left margin of the field, and centered in the height of the row. The left margin is (2 * first - others) / 2 of the margins that Table measures (6 pixels with the default font, 8 with 14.Helvetica), as the separator of the fields after the first is on their right. Checked on the screen with two fonts (2026-11-13).

The behavior that differs from Windows is in difference 014.
